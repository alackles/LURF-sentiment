Final interpretation:
This analysis treats VADER and NRCLex results as sentiment/framing indicators,
not as perfect measures of author attitude. The goal is to compare how emotional
and sentiment framing changes across time periods and keyword groups as the field
moves from older dialogue-system terminology toward newer chatbot and
conversational-AI terminology.

Small-n interpretation note:
The early-period sentiment estimates for some keyword groups should be interpreted cautiously. In 1976–1999, the chatbot/conversational-AI group has only 5 records, and the agent/assistant group has 26 records. This means the high early chatbot sentiment score is likely sensitive to a very small sample size rather than a stable field-wide pattern.
